# Documentation Revision Notes — Creative Direction v2

## Why this revision exists
The prior specifications could be interpreted as requiring too much whitespace, identical surface treatment, and overly prescriptive section compositions. This revision keeps the brand and functional boundaries while explicitly allowing richer art direction and agent-led design decisions.

## Main changes
- Opening-to-Hero transition target changed from 1.2–1.6 seconds to approximately 3–4 seconds, with meaningful asset movement/recomposition rather than fade-only transitions.
- Added explicit textured warm-ivory backgrounds and varied surface treatments.
- Added section-to-section variation in botanical asset placement, crop, scale, density, and framing.
- Clarified that key sections must not look accidentally empty, especially Opening, Hero, Venue details, and Gallery.
- Added a persistent realistic-sketch bird motif that changes position with scrolling, with accessibility and performance constraints.
- Changed section art direction from rigid compositions to a visual brief with creative latitude.
- Added QA criteria for visual richness, transition choreography, bird behavior, and section variation.
- Preserved hard requirements for wedding facts, RSVP behavior, security, privacy, and accessibility.
